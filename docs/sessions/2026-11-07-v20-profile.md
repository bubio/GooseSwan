## How the V20 differs from the 8086 (all measured, not from the manual)
- **Prefixes 64h/65h are string-repeat prefixes keyed on CF**: 64h repeats
  while CF=0, 65h repeats while CF=1 (the REPC/REPNC pair). F2/F3 keep their
  ZF meaning; all four repeat unconditionally for MOVS/STOS/LODS/INS/OUTS.
- **60-6B are the 80186 additions**, not Jcc aliases: PUSHA, POPA, BOUND
  (out-of-range takes INT 5 pushing the *next* instruction's IP), PUSH
  imm16/imm8, IMUL with immediate. 63h/66h/67h decode a ModRM and do nothing.
- **6C-6F are INS/OUTS** (INS stores the open-bus FF the rig presents).
- **C0/C1 are shift-by-imm8 groups**; **C8/C9 are ENTER/LEAVE**, and the
  V20's ENTER drops SP by a further 2×level below frame−disp (the display
  area is not folded into disp).
- **Shift /6 is SAL** (= SHL), not SETMO; **SHL always clears AF**.
- **D6 is XLAT** (as is D7); there is no SALC.
- **AAD ignores its immediate byte and always uses base 10**, with flags
  from the internal add ((AH×10) mod 256) + AL — verified exactly.
- AAM still honours its immediate; POPF normalization matches the 8086.

## Residue on the NEC side (new)
- **MUL's SF/ZF/PF are microcode residue on the V20** (CF/OF are exact) —
  the mirror image of the 8086, where they came cleanly from the high half.
  IMUL and IMUL-imm share the residue for their undefined flags; DIV/IDIV
  and AAM base 0 behave as on the 8086. These 11 files are the only
  non-green ones in either corpus now.
